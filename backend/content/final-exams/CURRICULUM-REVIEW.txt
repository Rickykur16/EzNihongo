The global module vocabulary list is not proof that a word was taught.
Review used words attached to lesson decks and the lesson support examples.
Inflected forms, taught counters/numbers, personal names and kana reading
errors are not treated as separate new vocabulary. Grammar distractors may
be incorrect applications of forms being tested; they need not be valid
sentences, but must use familiar components.

Examples of decisions:
- N5 comparison: replace N4 ほど with N5 Bab 18 より.
- N5 sequence: use Bab 12 てから instead of N4 Bab 3 たあとで.
- N5 plans/desire: replace N4 ことができる with Bab 19 つもり / たい.
- N5 requests: use Bab 13 permission/request forms, not untaught greetings.
- Replace obscure cleaning vocabulary and computer terminology with
  classroom/household words present in teaching material.
- N4: replace ず with taught ないで and fabricated connective/spelling
  distractors with familiar alternatives.

Implementation keeps shared passages, spoken choices, explanations and
answer IDs synchronized. Migration 199 preflights all edited bank rows,
refuses conflicts, and supersedes affected unfinished attempts. It does not
delete completed attempts or recompute their historical grades. Replaying
migration 199 does not supersede newly started corrected exams.

Validation: structural checks plus PostgreSQL HTTP regression tests cover
counts, A/B composition, unique choices, one key, synchronized audio,
scoring, atomic failure, stale draft rejection, fresh starts and historical
results. These checks prevent specified regressions; they are not an
independent linguistic certification of every possible interpretation.
